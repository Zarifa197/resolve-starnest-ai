# Resolve product review

Open https://resolve-starnest-ai.vercel.app/demo. No account, password, Shopify login, or payment is required to view the activity workspace.

The page shows actual Shopify test-store orders, their timestamps, source records, and the recorded response preview for the cancellation. Expand “View source record” to inspect the evidence.

The connection badge distinguishes a saved snapshot from a successful live Shopify read. Order updates refresh every 30 seconds when the server-side connection is configured. Browsing and cart collection are not connected to this workspace. Customer sending remains disabled; the recorded operator preview was accepted by Resend, without independently verified inbox delivery.

These are actual test-store records, not fabricated shopper histories. Test payments are not live charges. Instructions: https://resolve-starnest-ai.vercel.app/guide
